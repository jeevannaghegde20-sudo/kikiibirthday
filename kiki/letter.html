💌 A Letter I Never Said Out Loud

Kiki… 🤍

I don’t even know where to start, because there’s honestly so much I feel when it comes to you.

From the very first time we met while playing a game in drama practice to all the moments like bandhipur, class trip, temple run, Mumbai trip, that (cab ride 😅), and Goa… every memory with you stayed with me 💫

You are someone I admire deeply… your strength, your patience, everything about you 🌸

I may not always express things well… but I feel everything more than I show.

And maybe… this is something I never said — I tried to be there for you in my own way 🤍 And sometimes, when I didn’t feel you there… it stayed with me a little.

But even then… nothing changed how I see you.

I don’t need anything big… just your presence, your honesty, and those simple moments ✨

At the end of everything… I just want you to be happy 🤍

Because honestly… there’s no version of you that I wouldn’t choose 🌼

Happy Birthday, Kiki 🎂💖
You’ll always be special to me 🦋

— Bittu 🤍
